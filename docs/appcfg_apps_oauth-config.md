## appcfg apps oauth-config

Manage OAuth client credential configurations for your Gladly app platform apps

### Synopsis

The "oauth-config" command manages the OAuth client credentials (client_id and client_secret) used by Gladly app platform apps to authorize Gladly via the OAuth authorization_code grant. The credentials are associated with an authorization server by its auth domain.
